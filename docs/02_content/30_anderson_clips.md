# ANDERSON CLIP SCRIPTS

*Full text for the recorded Anderson clips. Anderson uses the hybrid presentation pattern from Sigma Protocol — primary clip on video, follow-up status clips in low-bandwidth packet format if needed.*

---

## ABOUT THESE CLIPS

Anderson is Command authority. He appears at three points in the mission:

1. **New Orders (Act II, Scene 5):** Anderson transmits the diversion order to investigate the distress signal. Primary clip on video. ~45-60 seconds.
2. **Status Acknowledgment (optional, Act III resolution):** Brief Command-level response to the rescue and pirate incident. Low-bandwidth packet format. ~20-30 seconds.
3. **Closing Acknowledgment (optional, debrief):** Final Command-level note. Low-bandwidth packet format. ~15-20 seconds.

Anderson's voice (from Sigma Protocol design): measured, formal, professionally distant. He does not editorialize. He does not warm. He gives orders and acknowledges outcomes. The procedural register is the point.

The negative-space guardrail applies here: Anderson does NOT mention quantum field stabilizers, the Khovan Reach cache as a fetch target, or any specific component. The cache is referenced only as regional context.

## CLIP 1: NEW ORDERS

**Revision note (operator-ratified 2026-08-16).** The qualification officer is **Commander Dillon**.

**Trigger:** Approximately one hour into the qualification cruise (after Drill Three concludes). Plays as a video transmission on the main screen.

**Setup:** Comms announces incoming priority transmission from Command. Anderson appears on screen.

**Script:**

> "Captain. Admiral Anderson, Command Coordination.
>
> Long-range monitoring has detected a fragmentary distress signal in the Khovan Reach region. The signal is on a TSN-compatible emergency band but has been too degraded for source identification or authentication.
>
> Pattern analysis suggests either a civilian emergency beacon or a possible TSN vessel in difficulty. We cannot resolve the source from Command range. No other TSN assets are positioned to respond within operational timeframes.
>
> Your orders, Captain: proceed to the Khovan Reach region, locate the signal source, render aid as appropriate, and report findings. Anticipated transit from your current position is approximately ninety minutes at standard cruise.
>
> The qualification framework remains in effect. This is now a live operation under Commander Dillon's observation. Operate accordingly.
>
> Command, out."

**Notes on delivery:**

- Pace: measured, not rushed. This is a senior officer giving operational orders, not an emergency call. Anderson is professional even when transmitting urgent material.
- Tone: formal throughout. No warmth, no editorializing. The "operate accordingly" is procedural, not pointed.
- Length: ~50 seconds at normal command-briefing pace. If you record faster, the urgency overcalibrates and the players feel rushed when they shouldn't.

**Production notes:**

- This is the only Anderson clip players will see at full video quality. Visual presence matters here — record with clean lighting, neutral background, dress uniform.
- If using stable-image-with-audio hybrid, the still image should be Anderson seated, slightly forward, regulation pose. Not casual.

## CLIP 2: STATUS ACKNOWLEDGMENT (optional, conditional)

**Trigger:** Plays once Artemis has resolved the Halcyon Drift situation and reported the pirate incident. May not be played at all — GM's choice based on session pacing.

**Setup:** Comms reports incoming priority packet from Command. The packet format implies degraded comms — the visual is a low-bandwidth still image with audio, not full video. Justification: containment-zone interference or routine packet routing.

**Script (clean rescue, no DAMCON losses):**

> "Cruiser Artemis, Command Coordination.
>
> Your report on the Khovan Reach incident is acknowledged. Civilian vessel Halcyon Drift stabilized; crew preserved. Pirate elements engaged and neutralized.
>
> Captain Hessler's vessel has been logged as protected status pending TSN escort to Vesperan territory. Pirate vessel disposition will be coordinated separately.
>
> Continue qualification framework through return transit. Full debrief on station arrival.
>
> Anderson, out."

**Script (with DAMCON losses):**

> "Cruiser Artemis, Command Coordination.
>
> Your report on the Khovan Reach incident is acknowledged. Civilian vessel Halcyon Drift stabilized; crew preserved. Pirate elements engaged and neutralized.
>
> Casualties among your DAMCON personnel are logged. Reyes, Park, Achebe — recorded with honors. Replenishment scheduled at next station rotation.
>
> Captain Hessler's vessel has been logged as protected status pending TSN escort to Vesperan territory.
>
> Continue qualification framework through return transit. Full debrief on station arrival.
>
> Anderson, out."

**Script (mission with Halcyon Drift loss):**

> "Cruiser Artemis, Command Coordination.
>
> Your report on the Khovan Reach incident is acknowledged. Civilian vessel Halcyon Drift is logged as lost. Surviving crew aboard Artemis. Pirate elements neutralized.
>
> Casualties among your DAMCON personnel, if any, are logged separately.
>
> The loss of a civilian vessel under TSN aid response is a serious matter. The incident will receive full Command review.
>
> Continue qualification framework through return transit. Full debrief on station arrival.
>
> Anderson, out."

**Notes on delivery:**

- Cooler than Clip 1. This is acknowledgment, not briefing. Anderson should sound like he's reading a report into a recorder.
- The DAMCON casualty acknowledgment line — "Reyes, Park, Achebe — recorded with honors" — is the one beat where Anderson's voice can slightly modulate. Not warm, but acknowledging. Read it cleanly.
- The "serious matter" line in the Halcyon Drift loss variant is firm but not punitive. Anderson is informing the captain that the inquiry is real, not lecturing them.

**Production notes:**

- Three variants of this clip needed: clean success, casualty acknowledgment, Halcyon Drift loss. Each ~25-30 seconds.
- If you want to reduce production load, record only the first two — the Halcyon Drift loss variant is the worst-case outcome that most groups will not hit.

## CLIP 3: CLOSING ACKNOWLEDGMENT (optional)

**Trigger:** Plays at the very end of the debrief, after Dillon's qualification review concludes. Optional — GM may choose to end with Dillon's closing instead.

**Setup:** Final Command transmission. Low-bandwidth packet format.

**Script:**

> "Cruiser Artemis, Command Coordination.
>
> Mission complete. Qualification cruise concluded under operational conditions. Your performance is logged.
>
> Crew assignments and follow-on orders will be transmitted within twenty-four standard hours.
>
> Command appreciates your work. Anderson, out."

**Notes on delivery:**

- Brief. ~15-20 seconds. This is the operational close.
- The "appreciates your work" line is the closest Anderson gets to commendation. Procedural acknowledgment, not warmth.

---

## PRODUCTION SUMMARY

If you record everything: 4-5 clips (Clip 1, three variants of Clip 2, Clip 3). About 3 minutes of total audio.

If you record minimum viable: 2 clips (Clip 1 + one variant of Clip 2). About 75 seconds of audio.

Anderson is the easier voice to record than Dillon — fewer clips, more uniform tone. You can probably record all of these in a single 30-minute session.
