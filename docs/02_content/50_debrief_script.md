# KHOVAN REACH — DEBRIEF SCRIPT TEMPLATE v2.2

*Template for the post-mission debrief delivered by Dillon. GM uses this as a structural guide; specific content comes from the v2.2 qualification cards and runtime support display filled in during play.*

**Revision note (operator-ratified 2026-08-16) — Dillon is never physically aboard Artemis.** He transmits from Kestrel Yards for the entire cruise, including this debrief. See `docs/01_design/00_scenario_play_guide.md` for the full design-canon correction.

---

## ABOUT THIS TEMPLATE

The debrief is approximately 10 minutes. It is the culmination of the mission — the moment where the qualification framework becomes visible to the players. Dillon walks through each station's observations, delivers results, and closes the cruise.

The template provides:

- Pre-recorded Dillon clip cues (when to play which clip)
- GM-voiced framing language for each section
- Station-by-station prompts for content the GM has tracked
- Special handling for DAMCON casualties if they occurred
- The closing sequence

The template is sequential. Work through it in order. Customize the GM-voiced content based on the actual qualification card observations.

## DEBRIEF FLOW

### Section 1: Mission close announcement (~1 min)

**Trigger:** Artemis is on return transit, all operational matters resolved.

**GM voice (as Captain announces return transit):**
> "Helm, set course for Tarsis. Standard cruise. Engineering, manage reserves for the return trip."

(Pause for Helm and Engineering to acknowledge.)

**Dillon's transmission opens on the bridge speakers:**

**Play Dillon Clip 10, Part 1 (opening segment):**
> "Captain. Crew. Khovan Reach is operationally complete. We're returning to standard. I'll walk the qualification framework station by station. Before I begin, the operational outcome:"

**GM voice (operational outcome summary — adapt based on session results):**

The GM speaks this in Dillon's tone. Examples by outcome:

**Clean success (no DAMCON loss, Halcyon Drift repaired, pirates neutralized):**
> "Halcyon Drift stabilized and crew preserved. Pirate elements engaged and neutralized — surrendered or destroyed. No own-ship casualties. Component recovery successful. Cruise objectives met operationally."

**DAMCON loss but mission otherwise successful:**
> "Halcyon Drift stabilized and crew preserved. Pirate elements engaged and neutralized. DAMCON casualties — three personnel lost in the cascade response. Component recovery successful. Halcyon Drift saved at cost of own-ship personnel."

**Halcyon Drift loss (rare):**
> "Halcyon Drift is logged as lost. Surviving Vesperan crew aboard Artemis pending escort to home territory. Pirate elements neutralized. DAMCON casualties — [number] personnel. Cruise objectives partially met."

**Play Dillon Clip 10, Part 2 (closing segment):**
> "Standard debrief order: Captain, Helm, Weapons, Engineering, Science, Comms. Begin with the Captain."

### Section 2: Station-by-station debrief (~7 min)

For each station, the GM voices Dillon's review in Dillon's procedural tone. Use the qualification card observations to drive content. Each station's review takes approximately 60-75 seconds.

The template structure for each station:

> "[Station name]. Observation review.
>
> [Observation 1 result with brief context]. [Observation 2 result with brief context]. [Observation 3 result with brief context]. [...]
>
> Overall: [Pass / Pass with development items / Pass with retest required / station-specific summary].
>
> [Optional: one specific developmental note if the station had standout work or specific gaps]."

### Captain debrief

**GM voice (as Dillon):**
> "Captain. Observation review.
>
> [State whether Captain ran the Act I fire-control sequence: guided posture/objective/ceasefire in Drill Two, then independent Engine objective/fire authorization/ceasefire in Drill Three.] [State whether captain made decisive deviation call.] [State whether captain recognized the convergence cleanly.] [State the engineer placement decision and whether it was committed.] [State whether torpedo conversion was clearly authorized or not.] [State whether pirate/salvage-cover handling was effectively delegated and timed.]
>
> Overall: [Pass / Pass with development items / Retest required].

> [Optional developmental note. Examples:]

### Section 3: DAMCON acknowledgment (conditional, ~1 min)

**Trigger:** Only if DAMCON casualties occurred (`damcon_outcome = total_loss`).

**Play Dillon Clip 11 (DAMCON Replenishment):**
> "DAMCON personnel status.
>
> Reyes, Park, and Achebe. Logged as deceased in the line of duty. Recorded with honors. Replenishment scheduled at next station rotation.
>
> This loss is part of the operational record. The crew should know who they were."

**GM voice (as Dillon, continuing):**

If specific decisions led to the loss:
> "The decision that produced the loss is recorded. Captain — your engineer placement and torpedo conversion choices produced the compressed timer scenario. The choice reflects what you knew under available information. The outcome reflects what was real about the situation. Both are part of the operational record."

If a chain of decisions or external factors contributed:
> "The cascading factors that produced the loss are recorded. The cache retry, the engineer placement, and the pirate/salvage-cover arrival timing all compressed the available window. No single decision was the cause. Worth reviewing as a system rather than assigning to one moment."

If the captain was visibly affected by the loss, acknowledge briefly:
> "Captain. The loss is real. So is the rest of the work the crew did. Halcyon Drift is going home because of decisions you made. The cost is also real."

(No further commentary. Move to closing.)

### Section 6: Open floor (~30 sec to several minutes)

After Dillon's "Dismissed" and Anderson's optional clip, the GM opens the floor to player questions.

**GM voice (in their own voice, not Dillon's):**
> "That's the end of the formal qualification. Any questions about your station, your performance, or the mission?"

This is where players can ask:
- Why a specific item was rated as it was
- What other decisions would have looked like
- How the scenario would have played differently with a different captain decision
- Process questions about the qualification framework

The GM answers in their own voice, not as Dillon. This is the moment the qualification framing relaxes and the table conversation begins.

If players have no questions, end the session cleanly.

If players have many questions, this section can run 10-15 minutes. Budget for it; don't rush. The debrief discussion is often where the most valuable learning happens.

## TONE NOTES FOR THE GM

When delivering the GM-voiced portions of the debrief in Dillon's tone:

- **Procedural, not warm.** Dillon doesn't congratulate. He acknowledges.
- **Brief.** Each observation is one or two sentences. No padding.
- **Specific, when possible.** "Your Drill Three Engine-disable pass used correct beam tuning and ceased fire immediately after the objective" lands better than "your weapons work was good."
- **Honest about gaps without lecturing.** "Your selection on the first cache attempt was incorrect" is a fact. "You should have known the difference between civilian-grade and military-grade" is a lecture.

When delivering the DAMCON acknowledgment if losses occurred:

- **Pause.** Read the names cleanly with a beat between each.
- **Don't soften.** Don't say "I'm sorry." That's the GM's voice, not Dillon's.
- **Don't escalate.** Dillon's procedural tone is the dignity. Adding emotion to the line breaks it.
- **Move forward without rushing.** When the clip ends, give the table 5-10 seconds before continuing. Players need a moment to absorb it.

When delivering the overall mission result:

- **Match the outcome.** A clean qualification gets a clean read. A retest-required outcome gets a more deliberate, slightly heavier read.
- **Don't apologize for retest requirements.** Dillon doesn't apologize. The retest is procedural; it's part of the framework.

When delivering the closing:

- **Read Dillon's "you're a crew now" line cleanly.** This is the most personal Dillon gets. Restraint is the point.
- **The "Dismissed" is final.** Stop there. Don't add. Anderson's clip, if used, follows separately.
