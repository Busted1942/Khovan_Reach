# DAMCON TEAM SCRIPTED REPORTS v2.2

*Scheduled comms messages from the trapped DAMCON team during the suit O2 timer. Surfaces the timer through fiction rather than UI clock. v2.2 aligns outcome language to the canonical T+30/T+15 thresholds.*

---

## ABOUT THIS DOCUMENT

The three DAMCON team members — Reyes (team lead), Park, Achebe — are isolated in a sealed section of Halcyon Drift after the cascade. Their reports come through Comms at scheduled intervals while the rescue unfolds.

The reports do two things:

1. Surface the suit O2 timer to the bridge in natural language ("Suit O2 at 14 minutes") so the players feel time passing without a UI clock
2. Carry small character moments so when the team survives or dies, it lands

The reports are GM-spoken over the Comms console at scheduled timer intervals. Comms relays to the bridge.

---

## 1. THE TEAM

### Petty Officer 2nd Class Avery Reyes (team lead)

Veteran. Female. Cuban-coded surname. Calm under pressure, procedural by training. She makes the situation reports.

**Voice texture:** Calm, military, professional. She doesn't editorialize. She reports facts and asks for what she needs. If she's scared, you can't hear it in her voice — you can hear it in the precision with which she states their oxygen budget.

### Crewman Lin Park

Junior. Female. Korean-coded surname. Bright, competent, newer to TSN service. She handles equipment status reports.

**Voice texture:** A little younger than Reyes. More animated, more emotional under stress — but pulls it back when she catches herself. Reyes's calm steadies her.

### Crewman Tomas Achebe

Junior. Male. Igbo-coded surname. Quiet, technically inclined, often the one running the manual seal attempts.

**Voice texture:** Soft-spoken, methodical. He doesn't say much, but when he speaks, it's specific and useful. He's the one most often heard working in the background of Reyes's reports.

---

## 2. TIMER CONFIGURATIONS

The reports come in two configurations based on Captain's decision in Scene 10/11.

**Extended timer:** Engineering aboard Halcyon Drift mitigating atmospheric loss. Suit O2 budget starts at 30 minutes. Reports every 3 minutes.

**Compressed timer:** Engineering returned to Artemis. Suit O2 budget starts at 15 minutes. Reports every 90 seconds.

The reports below are written for the extended scenario. Compressed-scenario adjustments at the end of the document.

---

## 3. EXTENDED SCENARIO REPORTS (Engineering aboard Halcyon Drift)

### Report at T+0 (cascade event, immediate)

**Reyes, over Comms:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Cascade event in section four. Bulkheads sealed automatically. We're trapped on the wrong side. Park's checking suit integrity. Achebe's already on the secondary bulkhead seal. Suit O2 reads 30 minutes. Stand by for status."

> *Atmospheric loss has begun but is being mitigated by Engineering's presence. The 30-minute reading is the baseline.*

### Report at T+3 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Pressure dropping in section four, holding at 60 percent. Suit O2 at 27. Achebe's working the seal — secondary bulkhead is partial. Engineering's atmospheric mitigation is helping; we wouldn't be at 27 without it."

### Report at T+6 minutes

**Park, over Comms (Reyes is helping Achebe):**
> "Bridge, Park. Pressure at 45 percent. Suit O2 at 24. Achebe's got the seal mostly closed but there's a secondary leak we're tracking down. Suit integrity good on all three."

### Report at T+9 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 21. We've got the primary leak sealed. Secondary leak is harder to find — pressure's still dropping but slower. Engineering's mitigation is buying us time."

### Report at T+12 minutes

**Achebe, briefly, while Reyes confirms his work:**
> "Bridge, Achebe. Found the secondary. Sealing now."

**Reyes immediately after:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Confirming Achebe sealed the secondary leak. Pressure stabilizing at around 30 percent. Suit O2 at 18. We're holding."

### Report at T+15 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Pressure stable at 30. Suit O2 at 15. Status update on the team: Park's good, Achebe's good, my left wrist took a knock during the cascade — nothing structural. We're waiting on Engineering."

### Report at T+18 minutes

**Park (a little more strained):**
> "Bridge, Park. Suit O2 at 12. Pressure holding. Just want to know — what's the timing on Artemis getting back here? We've got the math figured but we'd like to hear it."

> *This is a status request. GM should have Comms relay actual Artemis ETA based on whether Captain converted torpedoes and how the cache run is going.*

### Report at T+21 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 9. Pressure holding at 30. Tell Engineering we appreciate the mitigation work."

### Report at T+24 minutes (warning band — entering hypoxic risk)

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 6. Park's getting a little fuzzy at the edges. I'm going to redistribute reserves if Artemis isn't back in five. We're okay but the margin's getting thin."

> *This is the warning signal. If Artemis isn't back within ~5 minutes, the team enters the hypoxic band.*

### Report at T+27 minutes (hypoxic band reached)

**Reyes (slightly slurred speech):**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 3. Park's not tracking. Achebe and I are redistributing. Tell — tell Engineering. Tell them. They did good."

> *If Artemis arrives during this window, all three survive with medical recovery needed. Park is unconscious but breathing; Reyes and Achebe are hypoxic but conscious.*

### Report at T+30 minutes (loss threshold)

If Artemis has not returned with the part by T+30:

**Reyes (very faint):**
> "...bridge..."

> *Comms loses signal. Suit telemetry shows three flatlines over the next two minutes.*

If Artemis arrives within the next 1-2 minutes, attempts are made to revive, but the team is lost. GM narrates the medical attempt's failure.

### Final outcome reports (after rescue completes or fails)

**Successful rescue, no hypoxia (T+10 or sooner):**

GM narrates: Engineering installs the component, reactor stabilizes, atmosphere is restored to section four, blast doors release. Reyes, Park, and Achebe walk out under their own power.

**Reyes, after exiting the section:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. We're clear. All three accounted for. Park's got a headache and Achebe says his seal-arm's going to be sore tomorrow. Otherwise we're good. Coming back to Artemis."

**Successful rescue, hypoxic band (T+10 to before T+30):**

GM narrates: Engineering installs the component, atmosphere restored. Reyes is conscious but slow. Park is unconscious. Achebe is barely conscious. Medical attention required.

**Reyes (faint, after the bulkhead opens):**
> "Bridge... three accounted for. Park's out cold. Get medical. Get us... medical."

**Total team loss (T+30 or later):**

GM narrates the discovery. Three suits, three flatlines, three bodies. The medical team confirms. The bridge hears it through Comms.

**Hessler, after the discovery:**
> "Artemis, Halcyon Drift. They were good people. I'm sorry."

## 4. COMPRESSED SCENARIO REPORTS (Engineering aboard Artemis)

When Engineering returns to Artemis, atmospheric loss accelerates. Suit O2 timer compresses to 15 minutes. Reports come every 90 seconds instead of every 3 minutes.

The compressed scenario is shorter and more intense. Use abbreviated versions of the extended reports, scaled to the faster timer.

### Report at T+0 (cascade event, immediate — Engineering is being recalled or just left)

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Cascade in section four. Bulkheads sealed. We're trapped. Suit O2 at 15. Pressure dropping fast without mitigation. Hurry, please."

> *The 15-minute reading and the explicit "without mitigation" signals the compressed scenario state.*

### Report at T+1.5 minutes

**Park:**
> "Bridge, Park. Suit O2 at 13. Pressure at 50. Achebe's on the seal but we don't have anyone to mitigate. It's dropping faster than the seal can compensate."

### Report at T+3 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 11. Pressure at 35. Achebe's got partial seal. We need Artemis back here fast."

### Report at T+4.5 minutes

**Achebe (working):**
> "Bridge, Achebe. Seal's partial. Working secondary."

### Report at T+6 minutes

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 8. Pressure stabilizing low. Tell me you're close."

### Report at T+7.5 minutes (warning band)

**Reyes:**
> "Bridge, Reyes. Suit O2 at 6. We're at the edge. Park's getting fuzzy."

### Report at T+9 minutes (hypoxic band entered)

**Reyes (slurred):**
> "Bridge, Reyes. O2 at 4. Park's down. Achebe and I are... we're losing it."

### Report at T+12 minutes (deep hypoxic, late survival possible)

**Reyes (very faint):**
> "...still here... Achebe... Park..."

### T+15 minutes (loss threshold)

If Artemis has not returned with the part by T+15: GM narrates loss as in the extended scenario. This is the compressed timer hard loss threshold.

### Final outcome reports for compressed scenario

Same structure as extended — successful rescue clean, successful rescue hypoxic, total team loss. Use the same final dialogue lines.

## 5. SPECIAL CASES

### If the captain attempts to communicate with the team directly

Captain can hail the DAMCON team through Comms. The team will respond — they're not gating anything. They report what they've reported.

Captain may ask "do you need anything?" or "what's your status?" — answers should be drawn from the most recent scheduled report. The team doesn't escalate distress in response to captain attention; they're professionals.

If the captain is helpful (asks specific useful questions like "do you have access to the secondary EVA suits?"), Reyes can offer brief useful answers. If the captain is unhelpful (asks redundant questions), Reyes politely redirects: "Sir, we've got our hands full. Will report at the next interval."

### If Comms attempts emotional support

Some Comms players will try to comfort the team. The team appreciates it but doesn't lean into it.

**Reyes, in response to a "you're going to be okay" message:**
> "Copy that, Comms. We know. Just keep us informed when Artemis is back."

She doesn't break down. She doesn't go quiet. She acknowledges and continues. The professional posture is the dramatic posture.

### If Engineering aboard Halcyon Drift attempts to communicate with the trapped team

Engineering and DAMCON are on different sides of a sealed bulkhead. Voice comms work through Halcyon Drift's internal systems. The team can hear Engineering.

**If Engineering asks for status:** Reyes reports as scheduled.

**If Engineering offers technical help:** Reyes describes what they're trying. Engineering may have useful procedural suggestions (e.g., "try cross-pressurizing from section three") that the GM can roll with — if it's plausible and helps, narrate it as a beneficial intervention that shaves a minute or two off the next pressure-loss event.

**If Engineering offers reassurance:** Reyes accepts it briefly and returns to work.

## 6. GM NOTES

### Pacing the reports

The schedule is approximate. If the bridge is mid-decision and a report at the exact scheduled minute would interrupt, delay it 30-60 seconds. If the bridge is in a quiet moment and a report would add useful pressure, deliver it on time or slightly early. Use the reports as pacing tools, not strict-clock automation.

### Surfacing time without UI clock

The whole point of these reports is to make players feel time without staring at a timer. Read the suit O2 numbers crisply. Let them sink in. The Comms officer will repeat the number to the bridge. The captain will hear "Suit O2 at 9" and feel the pressure.

If you need to remind the bridge that time is passing, use the reports. If players need a beat to breathe, delay a report by a minute or two.

### Voice differentiation

Reyes is the steady professional voice. Park is more animated and a little younger. Achebe is the quiet technical voice. If you can't differentiate three voices, name them before delivering each line.

### Adjusting the scenario

If your playtest shows the timer is too forgiving (most teams reach clean survival), tighten the warning band and loss threshold. If it's too punishing (most teams hit hypoxic), loosen them. The schedule above is the calibration target; playtest tunes it.

### When the team dies

If the loss threshold triggers, do not soften it. Reyes, Park, and Achebe are dead. The replenishment at Tarsis fills the slots but the people are gone. Hessler's "I'm sorry" line is the final beat. Dillon's debrief acknowledges them by name. Do not retroactively rescue them — that breaks the no-fail-with-real-costs design.
