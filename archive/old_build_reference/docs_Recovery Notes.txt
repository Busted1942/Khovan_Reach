# 2026-05-17 Revert Notes - Drill 2 Recovery Attempt

## High-Level Changes Tried

- Revised Drill 2's Engineering walkthrough so the shield observation step became raise shields for 10 seconds, observe energy depletion, drop shields for 10 seconds, and observe the lower depletion.
- Added new Engineering walkthrough state flags and dev-jump seeding for those steps.
- Reworked the Drill 2 target several times
  - Started from the neutral training contact  TSN-style warpster target.
  - Tried a carrier-style or Kralien-style target so Science and Weapons would see normal hostile shield, hull, and subsystem data.
  - Added hostile-side setup, raider roles, fleet metadata, shieldhull tuning, and no-surrender handling.
- Removed or attempted to remove Science debuginitial scan clutter so Science would use normal `scan`, `status`, `intel`, and `bio` behavior.
- Shortened Science statusintel text after the status and intel panels became visually crowded.
- Changed Drill 2 controlled fire from a simple disable gate into a two-volley training sequence
  - Weapons targets the Weapons subsystem on the strongest shield frequency.
  - Science reports damage and relays the weakest shield frequency.
  - Weapons fires a second volley on the weak frequency.
- Added a weak-frequency relay confirmation button in Comms and staged damage handling that held Weapons around partial damage until the relay was confirmed.
- Tried to prevent Weapons from losing its lock after the target subsystem was disabled.
- Adjusted Drill 3 to use similar regular-hostile target behavior and preserve ceasefire after subsystem disable.
- Added additional objective text and comms broadcasts so the displayed goal would refresh after dev jumps and state transitions.
- Tried fallback Comms route changes for KestrelTarsis when menu options disappeared, then moved back toward the master-style KestrelTarsis route shape.

## What Broke Or Remained Unresolved

- KestrelTarsis Comms options disappeared or failed to populate reliably in this branch, even after restoring the route shape closer to `master`.
- The regular-hostile target approach produced confusing sidenameskin behavior and introduced surrendertaunt options.
- The target could expose normal Science data, but subsystem damage did not behave predictably enough for the training gate.
- Weapons damage could stall at scripted partial values, especially around the weak-frequency relay experiment.
- Some Science statusintel text still crowded the UI.
- The dev-jump objective display could show stale or wrong goals after jumping directly into later Drill 2 states.

## Lessons For A Fresh Attempt

- Start from `master` and make one narrow change at a time.
- Preserve the known-good KestrelTarsis Comms routes before changing target behavior.
- Treat target selection as the main risk. If normal subsystem damage is required, prefer a known stock enemyfightercarrier path that has already shown working subsystem damage in playtest.
- Avoid mixing three concerns in one pass Engineering walkthrough changes, target replacement, and Comms menu routing.
- If weak-frequency training returns, make it observational first; do not gate subsystem damage behind a new Comms relay until the baseline target reliably takes subsystem damage.
- Keep Science scan panel text short and let normal dynamic status data carry most of the information.
